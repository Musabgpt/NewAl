name: تقرير بحث
description: بحث بالنت من أكثر من مصدر وكتابة تقرير منظم مع المراجع
triggers: تقرير, بحث عن, ابحث عن, ملخص عن, report, research, مقارنة, قارن, أحدث, latest
---
- Search at least two different queries (Arabic and English) and read 2-4 good pages, not just snippets.
- Prefer primary and recent sources; note the date of each fact.
- Report: short summary first, then sections with bullet points, then "المصادر" with numbered links [1], [2]...
- Separate facts from opinions; say clearly when sources disagree or are missing.
- If the user wants a file, write the report as Markdown (.md) in the workspace and give its path.
